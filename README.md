# Needly Everyday Marketplace

Needly is a local everyday-services and commerce marketplace. The product is designed around one shared marketplace engine rather
than separate mini-apps for food, groceries, home services, auto, health,
utilities, education, hospitality, and local vendors.

The customer experience is organized around three transaction types:

- **Buy** — browse products, add to cart, pay, and receive delivery.
- **Book** — choose a service provider, schedule a date/time, confirm the
  location, and track the service.
- **Reserve** — choose a place, date/time, people or rooms, and receive a
  confirmation.

The current working implementation still focuses on the live Buy path:
food, groceries, local market, beverages, and pharmacy-style catalogues.
The home screen now presents the broader Needly scope and marks Book and
Reserve categories as planned service lines that require provider
onboarding, verification, scheduling, pricing, and location coverage rules.
